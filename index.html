FAQ

Good questions.

What does SteamlessController actually do?

It switches off the controller's built-in keyboard and mouse emulation, reads its raw input, and presents it to Windows as a standard Xbox 360 or PlayStation controller. Any game that supports those gamepads then works with it.

Can I keep Steam open?

Yes. You can toggle Steamless Mode without closing Steam. While Steamless Mode is on, the controller is yours; turn it off to give it back to Steam.

Which connection types work?

All three: the wireless puck, a USB cable, and Bluetooth LE (pair the controller in Windows Bluetooth settings first).

What is ViGEmBus?

A widely used open-source Windows driver that lets apps create virtual game controllers. The SteamlessController installer includes it and checks that it installed correctly.

Does it collect any data?

No. It sends nothing about you or your system. The remapping window loads its fonts from Google Fonts, and a missing-driver notification can open the ViGEmBus download page in your browser. That is all.
