                  https://github.com/JetBrains/JetBrainsMono

The font files are the unmodified upstream TrueType files (Barlow Regular, Medium,
SemiBold and Bold; JetBrains Mono SemiBold). The OFL requires the license text to
travel with them, so the installer copies both OFL files next to the program.
Do not rename or subset the fonts without reading the license first.
